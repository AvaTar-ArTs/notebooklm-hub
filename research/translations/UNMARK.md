# Unmark — English Capability Summary and Boundary

Unmark is a local browser tool for removing fixed NotebookLM export marks from PDF and PPTX files. Its PDF approach detects repeated lower-corner pixels across pages, narrows the mask to glyph shapes, and aborts when the match is too large or does not resemble a label. PPTX files may contain either removable shape objects or full-page raster images.

This is documented for awareness only. The Hub does not include watermark removal because ownership, attribution, export terms, and user-rights requirements need an explicit policy before implementation.
