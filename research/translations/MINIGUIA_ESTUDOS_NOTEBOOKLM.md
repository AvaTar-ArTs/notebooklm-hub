# Miniguia de Estudos com NotebookLM — English Capability Summary

This Portuguese study guide explores generative AI as a support for active learning. It recommends curating reliable sources, loading them into NotebookLM, designing reusable prompts, comparing simple/contextual/structured prompt styles, and recording limitations rather than treating generated answers as unquestionable truth.

The transferable lesson is a research workflow: source selection → grounded interrogation → prompt comparison → critical review → reusable study artifact. This fits the Hub’s provenance-first research skill and should remain guidance, not a claim that NotebookLM replaces subject expertise.
