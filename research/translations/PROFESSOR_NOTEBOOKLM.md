# Professor NotebookLM — English Capability Summary

Professor is a Claude Code study tutor for competitive-exam preparation. It combines three knowledge bases that are usually disconnected: NotebookLM study notebooks, real exam questions with answer keys, and personal notes about method and mistakes.

Its four-layer model is: a map of subjects and exam weight, notebook summaries and guides, categorized question banks, and an Obsidian-like vault of classes and error notes. A generated skill consults these layers in a fixed order and answers in the exam board’s style, including incidence, traps, and real-question references.

The Hub should treat this as a specialized skill over shared source, citation, memory, and artifact contracts—not as a new core backend.
