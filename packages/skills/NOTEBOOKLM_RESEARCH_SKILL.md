# NotebookLM Research Skill

This is the Hub-facing entry point for the reusable NotebookLM research
workflow. The detailed skill contract is maintained at:

[`notebooklm-research/SKILL.md`](notebooklm-research/SKILL.md)

Use it for source-grounded research, citation capture, evidence classification,
checkpointing, and artifact lineage. Live provider operations remain behind the
backend contract; this skill must not embed credentials or assume a mutable
shared NotebookLM session.
